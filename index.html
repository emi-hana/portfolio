About

I'm a fourth-year Psychology student at UCLA interested in the intersection of psychology and self-expression. Painting, especially portraiture, is how I explore that space.

I am also interested in cognition, behavior, mental health, and the application of psychological research to complex human problems.

In my free time, I enjoy philosophy and travel. See my blog, The Wandering Mind, to follow my journey.
